## Unraveling the Possibility of Reversing Gray Hair
Graying hair, medically referred to as canities or achromotrichia, has long been accepted as an inevitable part of the aging process.  Coloration of scalp hair has social and psychological importance across cultures, often symbolizing youth, vitality, and beauty.  When individuals seek solutions to gray hair, most often they rely on topical hair dyes—ranging from temporary color rinses to permanent dyes.  However, these come with downsides such as possible allergic reactions and damage to the hair shaft.  The search for a medication or therapy that can truly reverse the underlying process of hair graying has persisted for decades.  Recent reports have stirred interest in a phenomenon termed “medication-induced hair repigmentation,” wherein certain prescription drugs appear to cause gray or white hair to darken again
